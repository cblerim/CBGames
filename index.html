About me

I’m CBLERIM, an independent developer building games from prototype to playable release. I like getting deep into the systems: movement, physics, AI, multiplayer, atmosphere and the details that make a game feel real.

I'm most interested in VR, and especially in horror, where being physically inside the scene changes how a player reacts.

My projects don’t all fit one genre. One might be a story-driven VR sandbox, another a multiplayer horror game, and another a Roblox game built around exploration and collecting.

I develop in Unity and build for standalone headsets and PC.

Different games. Same developer.

Some are playable, some are still being built. Here’s what’s on my desk.

Become The Monster

In development

One player becomes the monster. Everyone else has to survive. Built around Gorilla Tag-style movement, physical interactions, distinctive monsters, varied maps and multiplayer tension. I want every round to create its own stories — especially when proximity voice chat turns a quiet hiding spot into a terrible idea.

Content warning: This game is intended for horror fans and features heavy gore, graphic violence and intense horror content.

Meta Quest 3 / 3S PCVR Unity 2021 Photon PUN 2 Multiplayer

Karlson VR

Original project

A VR sandbox built around physical interaction, movement and a strange story involving NEXUSOS. I’m interested in making the world react to the player instead of feeling like a set of scripted rooms. KARLSON VR is a personal project built through experimentation with a distinct visual identity.

Content warning: Horror themes and heavy gore may appear in this game.

Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

This policy explains what this website does and what data may be processed when you play my games. I aim to collect as little personal data as practical. Each game and platform can work differently, so please read the details below.

This website

This site is a static page. It has no accounts, no comment boxes, no contact forms, no advertising, and I don't run analytics or tracking scripts on it. I don't set cookies or collect personal information through the site itself.

The page loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them, and Google may receive your IP address as part of that request. Google's own privacy policy applies to that connection. Links to other sites, such as stores or social platforms, are governed by those sites' policies.

My games

  • Become The Monster is a multiplayer game that uses Photon PUN 2 for networking. To connect players, Photon processes technical data such as your IP address, a player or room identifier and in-game network messages. This is handled under Photon's privacy policy. I don't use that connection to build profiles of players or to sell data.
  • VR headsets track your head and hand movement to run a game. That data is used on your device to render the game and is not something I collect or store.
  • Karlson VR and Phobiamonke: if any of my games begin collecting data beyond what the platform you installed them from already does, I will describe it here before it ships.

Storefronts and headset platforms (for example Meta) have their own data practices for accounts, purchases and crash reports. Those are outside my control and are covered by the platform's policy.

My projects have different audiences, but some games are intended for mature horror audiences and are not designed for young children. Check the relevant store page and age rating before playing. These warnings describe the content; they do not replace the platform’s rating system or local age requirements.

I do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 through this website. Game platforms and third-party services may have their own age rules and data practices.
